BEFORE YOU COMMIT CAPITAL

Ask Better Questions First.

A strong opportunity can still become a weak decision when the structure is wrong. WIN starts before the transaction.

01

Have you thought it through?

Define the objective, the upside and the reason this deserves your attention.

02

How should it be funded?

Compare financing options, liquidity requirements, credit impact and flexibility.

03

What does the market say?

Understand competition, timing, demand and the forces that could change the outcome.

04

What is the timeline?

Know the milestones, pressure points and when capital must be available.

05

What happens after funding?

Capital solves one constraint. It should not create three new ones.

06

Can it scale?

Look beyond the first transaction and design for repeatability, resilience and growth.
